# Expand year coverage through verified stages

easyNIS aims to cover every released NIS year, but schemas, coding systems, sampling designs, and available validation data differ across years. Saad chose staged coverage with an explicit support matrix rather than delaying every release until all years are validated or treating readable files as evidence of complete support. Each year therefore needs recorded schema, import, survey, and applicable workflow evidence before its support claim is promoted.
