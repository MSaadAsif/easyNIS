- Studies created by `nis_new_study()` generate editable Methods and an
  HCUP/Khera checklist receipt from the opened sample, survey design, and
  rendered results. The receipt flags pre-design filtering and release-check
  discrepancies. Built-in tables apply disclosure protection, including checks
  for hidden dichotomous levels and complementary masking in cohort flow.
  All report estimates use the same analysis cohort.
  A shared disclosure check covers missing and complementary cells across
  outputs. Weighted cohort flow uses only valid-design discharges.
